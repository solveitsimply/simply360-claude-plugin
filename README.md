# Simply360

Connect your existing Simply360 account to read permitted record fields, create and update records, and manage supported workflows, forms, websites, files, reports and Team configuration. Each operation is an individually described tool. Your selected Team, enabled features, current permissions and approved OAuth scopes determine access. Reviewed proposals and sensitive-action confirmations remain in place.

Sign in with Simply Login when prompted. Approve read and write scopes for your intended Team. Reconnect an older read-only grant to approve additional scopes; existing grants are never silently expanded. Revoke access in Simply360 User Account > Connected Apps.

Try these examples in a Team and Collection where you have the required permissions:

1. “Find records in my selected Team.”
2. “Help me create a record in this Collection after checking its Fields.”
3. “Find the record I just created and update its Name, then archive it when I confirm.”

You can also ask “Prepare a reviewed change to my Team configuration.” Inspect schema before assigning Field values and report actual tool outcomes.

The selected AI provider receives requested tool results and OAuth credentials required for the connection. Share only information you are authorized to provide. Claude also processes this information under [Anthropic’s Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
